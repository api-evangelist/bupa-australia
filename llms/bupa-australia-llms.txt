# Bupa Australia
> Bupa Australia's core business is private health insurance for Australian residents, including hospital, extras and combined cover, as well as Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) and Overseas Visitors Cover (OVC). Bupa also offers general insurance (home, car, pet, landlords, travel, and phone & cyber) and corporate health cover through this site. This file highlights Bupa's principal health insurance, general insurance, product-selection and consumer guidance pages.
